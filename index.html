      1,000 books in 1,000 weeks

      Dr. Deas Virtual Library

      A reading log you can walk through. Every book stands on the spiral in the order it was read.

      Getting around

      Look
      Drag with the mouse or a finger
      Walk
      W A S D, the arrow keys, or the on-screen stick. Hold Shift to hurry.
      Follow the ramp
      Hold Space to walk up the spiral and C to walk down. It steers for you.
      Open a book
      Click or tap its cover. ← → step through the log while a book is open, Esc closes it.
      Jump
      Search by title or author, or click anywhere on the bar at the bottom to travel to that point in the log.
      Start over
      R returns you to the entrance.
